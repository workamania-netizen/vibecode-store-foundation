We love hearing how our customers use our honey — and some of the most creative uses go way beyond sweetening tea. Here are seven of our favorite unexpected ways to cook with honey.

## 1. Hot Honey Pizza Drizzle

Our **Hot Honey** was practically made for this. Drizzle it over a pepperoni pizza fresh from the oven. The sweet-spicy combination with salty cheese and crispy crust is absolutely addictive. Trust us — once you try it, plain pizza will never be the same.

## 2. Honey-Garlic Salmon Glaze

Mix our **Garlic Honey** with a splash of soy sauce and brush it on salmon fillets before broiling. The honey caramelizes beautifully, creating a sticky, savory-sweet glaze in about 10 minutes. The fermented garlic adds an umami depth that bottled garlic can't match.

## 3. Whipped Honey Butter

Beat softened butter with our **Cinnamon Honey** until fluffy. Spread it on warm biscuits, cornbread, or pancakes. You can make a batch and keep it in the fridge for up to two weeks — if it lasts that long.

## 4. Honey Vinaigrette

Whisk together equal parts **Wildflower Honey** and apple cider vinegar, then slowly drizzle in olive oil. Add a pinch of salt and a crack of black pepper. It's the simplest salad dressing you'll ever make, and it beats anything from a bottle.

## 5. Honey-Roasted Vegetables

Toss root vegetables (carrots, sweet potatoes, parsnips) with olive oil, salt, and a generous drizzle of **Clover Honey** before roasting. The honey caramelizes at the edges, creating those crispy, sweet-savory bits everyone fights over.

## 6. Honey Lemonade

Dissolve **Lavender Honey** in warm water, then mix with fresh lemon juice and cold water. The lavender adds a floral note that transforms simple lemonade into something special. Perfect for summer afternoons on the porch.

## 7. Honey-Glazed Bacon

Brush strips of thick-cut bacon with **Hot Honey** during the last few minutes of baking. The result is sweet, spicy, smoky, salty bacon that works as a breakfast side, a burger topping, or honestly, just a snack.
