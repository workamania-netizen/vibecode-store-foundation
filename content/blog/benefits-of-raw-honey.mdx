Most people think of honey as just a natural sweetener — a healthier alternative to sugar for your tea or toast. But raw, unfiltered honey like ours is so much more than that. Here are five benefits that might surprise you.

## 1. Packed with Antioxidants

Raw honey contains a wide range of antioxidants, including flavonoids and phenolic acids. These compounds help protect your cells from damage caused by free radicals, which are linked to aging and chronic diseases. Darker honeys like our **Buckwheat Honey** tend to have even higher antioxidant levels.

## 2. Natural Energy Boost

The natural sugars in honey — a balanced mix of glucose and fructose — provide quick, sustained energy without the crash you get from refined sugar. Many athletes use honey as a natural pre-workout fuel. A tablespoon of our **Wildflower Honey** before a hike or run can make a real difference.

## 3. Soothes Sore Throats

This one your grandmother probably knew: a spoonful of honey can calm a cough and soothe a sore throat. Studies have shown that honey can be as effective as some over-the-counter cough suppressants. Mix a tablespoon with warm water and lemon for a classic remedy.

## 4. Supports Digestive Health

Raw honey contains prebiotics — compounds that feed the beneficial bacteria in your gut. It also has mild antibacterial properties that can help keep your digestive system balanced. Many of our customers swear by a daily spoonful of raw honey for digestive comfort.

## 5. Rich in Enzymes and Nutrients

Unlike processed honey, raw honey retains all of its natural enzymes, vitamins, and minerals. These include small amounts of B vitamins, vitamin C, calcium, and iron. The key is that the honey is never heated above natural hive temperatures — which is exactly how we handle every batch at Martinsburg Honey Co.
